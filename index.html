04 — FAQ

Questions
creators ask.

Something else? Write to hello@getknowble.com.

How is the revenue split?+

You earn from 30% of net revenue – what’s left after Whop’s fees, refunds, chargebacks and any taxes Whop charges – depending on the project and your role in it. Your share is paid automatically to your own Whop account.

Does it cost me anything?+

No. There’s no upfront cost. We invest in the research and the product.

What do you expect from me?+

You know your audience best, so how you share it is up to you. A good start is one video and a few stories in launch week, then mentions whenever they fit. You only publish what you’ve approved.

Who handles customers?+

We do: delivery, customer service, refunds and updates.

Can I see a product before we talk?+

Yes. Every product page has a free preview of the first pages. Partners get the full product after signing a short confidentiality agreement.

Is it exclusive?+

No. We only ask that you don’t promote a competing guide on the same topic during our agreement.
