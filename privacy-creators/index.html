Privacy Notice for Creators

Knowble — creators we contact and creators we work with

Version 1.1 · Effective from 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

1.1 The controller of your personal data is Marcin Wucke, conducting business activity in Poland under the brand “Knowble”, address: 98-100 Łask, Poland, email: hello@getknowble.com (“we”). Contact us at that email with any privacy question or request.

1.2 This Notice is for creators: creators we contact about a possible partnership (section 2) and creators who sign a partnership agreement with us (section 3). If you buy one of our products, our Privacy Policy for customers applies, available at checkout in the Whop store.

2. Creators we contact about a partnership

2.1 Where we found you and what we keep. We look for creators whose public profiles on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube relate to the topics of our products. We record only public information relevant to a possible partnership: name or handle, profile links, audience size and topics, the contact email published on your profile, and our correspondence with you.

2.2 Why and on what basis. We rely on our legitimate interest in identifying potential business partners and assessing whether a collaboration may be relevant (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We limit the information to publicly available professional profile information and relevant correspondence.

2.3 Saying no. You can object at any time by replying “no” to our message or writing to hello@getknowble.com; we will then stop contacting you.

2.4 How long. If no partnership follows, we delete your data no later than 12 months after our last contact. If you object, we promptly delete your other data and keep only your email address and the date of your objection on a do-not-contact list, for as long as needed to respect your request. We review this list periodically.

3. Creators who partner with us

3.1 If you sign a partnership agreement with us, we process your name, contact details, social-media handles, address, the materials you provide (including your image), your Whop username for payouts, the tax documents needed for payments (such as a certificate of tax residence or a W-8BEN or W-9 form) and our correspondence.

3.2 We process this data to conclude and perform the agreement, including using your image as agreed in it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); to meet tax and accounting obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)); and to establish or defend legal claims (Art. 6(1)(f)). Your permission to publish your image is governed separately by the partnership agreement.

3.3 We keep this data for the term of the agreement and afterwards for as long as required by tax law or until related claims are time-barred.

3.4 We never share our customers’ personal data with partners; you see aggregated sales figures only.

5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

5.1 Google, Anthropic and Whop are based in the United States or process data there. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards available under the GDPR for the relevant service, such as an adequacy decision (including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients) or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. You can ask us for information about the safeguards that apply.

6. Your rights

6.1 Under the GDPR, and subject to the conditions it sets, you have the right to: access your data and receive a copy; have it corrected; have it erased; restrict its processing; receive it in a portable format (for data processed on the basis of a contract); and object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

6.2 To exercise your rights, email hello@getknowble.com. We reply within one month; in complex cases this may be extended as allowed by law, and we will tell you why.

6.3 You may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live or work or where an alleged infringement took place. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl. If you live outside the EEA and your local law gives you additional rights, contact us and we will handle your request under that law.

7. Changes

7.1 We may update this Notice. The current version is always available at the link in our messages and on request by email.
